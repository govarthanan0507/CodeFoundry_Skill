# Pre-Planning Block V2 — Standalone

## Purpose
Evaluate whether an approved product deserves progression into Design Council, using the completed Concrete Product Definition and Feasibility Assessment as evidence.

## Independence
No runtime dependency on CodeFoundry SHARED/, AGENTS/, Orchestrator, or another council.

## Required input
- concrete-product-definition.md, including appended Feasibility Assessment
- supporting evidence only when explicitly supplied

Pre-Planning does not redo Product Definition or Feasibility.

## Outputs
Exactly:
- pre-planning-assessment.md
- pre-planning-decision.md

## Verdicts
GO_TO_DESIGN_COUNCIL
NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE
RETURN_TO_PRODUCT_DEFINITION
RETURN_TO_FEASIBILITY
DO_NOT_PROCEED

Human authorization is required before Design Council.

## Boundary
Product Definition = what.
Feasibility = whether/how under known conditions.
Pre-Planning = whether progression deserves investment/attention.
Design Council = how the approved product should be shaped and designed.
