# Pre-Planning — Debate Protocol

This council is an atomic test unit. Test the council by supplying this folder; its member roles are internal components of the council, not separate test targets unless explicitly requested.

## Sequence
1. Advocate states the strongest honest case for building.
2. Skeptic states the strongest honest case against building.
3. Feasibility & Cost Assessor provides technical feasibility and at-scale cost evidence.
4. Market/Research provides competitor, market, and OSS evidence where relevant.
5. Advocate and Skeptic challenge claims using the evidence, not unsupported opinion.
6. A Synthesizer resolves the debate into GO, NO-GO, or NEEDS MORE EVIDENCE.
7. If the evidence is genuinely insufficient, the missing evidence is named rather than guessed.
8. Human approval is required before Planning begins.

## Test boundary
The council output is the decision package. A council performance test must evaluate the end-to-end decision, not merely whether individual role responses sound good.
