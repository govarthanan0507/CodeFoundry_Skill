# Market Research Report — Template

Produced by: Market/Research role (`roles/Market-Research.md`)
Always created — see "Fast-path" below. Never omitted.

## Header (always filled)

- Project / idea reference (`idea.md` link)
- Date
- Path taken: **FULL** or **FAST-PATH**
- If FAST-PATH: reason (must name the specific signal from `idea.md`'s
  personal/experimental/commercial context field — e.g. "personal/hobby
  project, no stated distribution or competitive intent")

## Fast-path rule

Every section below is still present in the document on fast-path — none
are deleted or omitted. Each is filled with:
`Not performed in full — [reason from Header]. Reuse/OSS check below is
the one exception and still runs in full regardless of path (see
SHARED/REUSE_AND_LICENSE_RULE.md).`

## Sections (full path)

### 1. Research goals
What this research needs to answer, and why it matters to the GO/NO-GO
decision.

### 2. Target audience / segments
Who the product is for, and how that maps to `idea.md`'s target user.

### 3. Competitive landscape
Named, real competitors and close alternatives — verified against what
they actually do today, not their marketing copy (per
`roles/Market-Research.md` Rule 1). Table: name, what it does, how it's
priced, closest overlap with this idea, gap it doesn't cover.

### 4. OSS / reuse candidates (never skipped, even on fast-path)
Repo Analyzer output for the idea's core mechanic. If none run:
explicit `NOT RUN` with reason (only valid reason: Tier 1 task per
`SHARED/PROCESS_SCALING.md` — see `SHARED/REUSE_AND_LICENSE_RULE.md`
Part 1). Any license finding recorded here in full, regardless of path
or tier (`SHARED/REUSE_AND_LICENSE_RULE.md` Part 2).

### 5. Market trends
Only what's relevant to whether this idea's timing makes sense — not a
general industry trends dump.

### 6. Consumer / user behavior
What target users currently do instead (informal alternatives — a
spreadsheet, a manual workaround, a competitor's free tier) — often the
real baseline this idea is competing against.

### 7. Opportunities & challenges
What this idea could exploit that alternatives don't; what could work
against it.

### 8. Evidence strength labels
Every finding above tagged: verified fact / unverified assumption /
inference / unknown (per `AGENTS/Ideation/AGENT.md` §7 discipline).

### 9. Recommendation to Advocate/Skeptic
One paragraph: what this research means for the debate, stated plainly,
not left for the Advocate/Skeptic to reinterpret.
