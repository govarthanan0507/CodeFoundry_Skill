# Debate Protocol — Design Council V2

Each applicable role states position/evidence; roles challenge; challenged roles defend/revise/reject; PM protects product meaning/structure; Synthesizer records decision, tradeoff and rejected alternative; Independent Critic attacks synthesis; material findings are resolved or carried visibly to human gate.

NO SYNTHESIS POSSIBLE is used when hard constraints conflict and no option satisfies them without a human-owned tradeoff.

INFEASIBLE PREMISE is used when the requested premise cannot be achieved as stated regardless of ordinary resource tradeoffs.

Ask users only when product intent is materially affected, requirements are genuinely incomplete, no reasonable default exists, or a human-owned preference cannot safely be inferred.
