# Runtime Contract — Design Council V2

## Entry
Require complete Concrete Product Definition + Feasibility Assessment, Pre-Planning assessment/decision with GO_TO_DESIGN_COUNCIL, and explicit human authorization.

## Roles
System/Product Architect; UX Designer; UI/Visual Designer; Frontend Architect; Backend/API Architect; Data/Database Architect; Security/Reliability Architect; Product Manager.

## Capability-by-capability
Preserve outcome/meaning; identify actors and behavior; identify dependencies; debate alternatives; identify functional and technical implications; resolve epic boundary with PM.

## Boundaries
Do not redo Feasibility. Do not redefine Product Definition. Do not let technical layers become product epics without product-value/enabling rationale.

## Traceability
UPSTREAM CAPABILITY → PRD EPIC → FRD BEHAVIOR → TRD DESIGN
