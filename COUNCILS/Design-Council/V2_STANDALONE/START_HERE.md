# Design Council V2 — Standalone

## Purpose
Turn an approved, feasibility-validated product into a coherent product structure and design package.

## Required input
- complete concrete-product-definition.md with Feasibility Assessment appended
- pre-planning-assessment.md
- pre-planning-decision.md with GO_TO_DESIGN_COUNCIL
- explicit human authorization

## Outputs
PRD.md — Product Manager
FRD.md — Design Council
TRD.md — Design Council

## Structure
Product Manager: Capability → Epic → Version
Product Owner downstream: Epic → User Story → Acceptance Criteria

## Independence
No runtime dependency on SHARED/, AGENTS/, Orchestrator, or another council.
